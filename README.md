# EX No.5.1.DESIGN THE 3D MODEL AS PROVIDED AND CONVERT TO ORTHOGRAPHIC VIEW

## AIM: 
To create orthographic view of given 3D model

## REQUIREMENTS: 
1. Autodesk fusion 360
2. Windows 10
3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 1 GB or more)
4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 

## PROCEDURE:
## Step 1
Create a new design and select the XY plane. 
Draw the base outline with an overall length of 120 mm and width of 120 mm as per the given drawing.

<img width="830" height="535" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8674d533-c216-4719-a564-e0f724c99f49" />

## Step 2
Extrude the base sketch to a thickness of 15 mm to create the bottom flange.
Create four mounting holes of Ø12 mm at the specified positions on the base plate using the Circle and Extrude Cut commands.

<img width="242" height="350" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/655725e6-60c3-461e-b4c9-65bab2ebbc60" />


## Step 3
Draw concentric circles of Ø46 mm, Ø40 mm, Ø20 mm and Ø10 mm at the centre of the base plate to create the cylindrical boss and central opening.

<img width="426" height="431" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7525ba88-41c2-45ca-ad7f-f1be739982a3" />

## Step 4
Extrude the central cylindrical portion to the required height of 40 mm and create the inner stepped portion using the Extrude Cut command.
<img width="242" height="350" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8c143c87-fc90-48f1-8d01-ae33e95110c7" />

## Step 5
Create the supporting ribs between the central boss and the base plate according to the given drawing.
Maintain the required overall height of 85 mm.
## Step 6
Apply the required R2, R3, R10 and R12 fillets to the edges to obtain a smooth finish.

<img width="202" height="552" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a6447d89-c6a6-4c06-b365-ae0d8671f1db" />

Check all dimensions, complete the 3D model and save the design.
